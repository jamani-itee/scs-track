1. What I set out to do: Build a three-tab Google Sheets inventory back office (Lists, Records, Dashboard) with data validation, range protection, and an owner's reference card for a kiosk.
2. What I did, in order: Ran the overkill test, structured Lists, Records, and Dashboard, added Reject-the-input validation rules, wrote whole-column SUMIFS formulas, protected the Dashboard and formula column, and ran hand checks and Break-the-Books drills with my partner.
3. What went wrong, and how I fixed it: Several dates in Records were entered in MM/DD/YYYY format instead of DD/MM/YYYY, causing Google Sheets to treat them as text or the wrong month and throwing off monthly totals; I retyped them in day-first format and verified every row.
4. How long it took, against the plan: About 45 minutes longer than planned because troubleshooting the mixed date formats and completing the full hand-check log took extra passes.
5. One lesson for the next person: Always set the spreadsheet locale first and verify that every date aligns right as a true date value before trusting monthly SUMIFS totals.
6. What I might be wrong about, and how I would find out: I assumed the kiosk owner will remember to add a correcting row instead of overwriting past entries or leaving blank prices, and I would find out by checking File > Version history and reviewing the Records tab with the owner after their first week of live use.
