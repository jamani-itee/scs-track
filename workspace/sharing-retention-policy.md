## 1. Where files live

All active business records, customer lists, price sheets, and operational templates live in company-owned Shared Drives rather than individual My Drive folders. Personal My Drive folders are used only for temporary drafts before moving files into the team Shared Drive.

## 2. Sharing outside the business

External file sharing is restricted by default so staff cannot share internal folders with outside addresses without manager approval. Default link sharing is set to Restricted (specific people only), and anyone with the link access is disabled for company Shared Drives.

## 3. Who can sign in

Only active staff with assigned accounts inside our organizational units may sign in, and 2-Step Verification (2SV) is enforced for every user. Mobile devices accessing Workspace data must enforce a screen lock passcode, and backup recovery codes are kept offline on paper rather than stored in files.

## 4. What we keep, how long, where

Active business records remain in Shared Drives while in use, while deleted Drive items stay in Trash for 30 days before permanent purge (admin restore window is 25 days after permanent deletion). Legal and tax record retention periods are to be checked with the owner and accountant before setting fixed multi-year rules.

## 5. The monthly export

On the first working day of each month, the admin exports a full Users CSV list and runs a Google Takeout archive of critical Shared Drive and business folders into offline encrypted storage (`company-backup.tar.gz`). Each monthly export is verified by restoring at least one test file into a staging folder.

## 6. Who holds admin

Only the business owner holds Super Admin rights with hardware or app-based 2SV and paper backup codes stored securely. Daily user and password support uses a delegated Help Desk or User Management Admin role so routine tasks never expose billing or full tenant deletion.

## 7. When someone leaves

When a staff member leaves, the admin immediately resets their password, revokes sign-in cookies, and suspends (never deletes) their account so no data is lost. Ownership of their Drive files is transferred to their manager, group memberships are removed, and email forwarding or archival is completed before any long-term license change.
